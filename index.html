Lower limb

Femoral Fractures

Explore femoral anatomy, fracture patterns and general fixation concepts.

Learning objectives

  • Identify the proximal, shaft and distal femur.
  • Understand common femoral fracture classifications.
  • Recognise broad fixation concepts.
  • Understand the importance of associated injuries.

Educational concepts

Femoral fractures may involve the proximal femur, femoral shaft or distal femur. Treatment planning depends on fracture location, morphology, patient factors and associated injuries.

Theatre learning

Study positioning principles, imaging requirements, implant familiarity and instrument preparation according to the planned procedure and local protocol.

Lower limb

Tibial Fractures

Learn about tibial shaft, plateau and distal tibial fracture patterns.

Learning objectives

  • Understand proximal, shaft and distal tibial anatomy.
  • Explore common fracture classifications.
  • Understand broad fixation options.
  • Recognise soft tissue considerations.

Educational concepts

Tibial injuries range from shaft fractures to complex articular injuries involving the plateau or distal tibial surface. Soft tissue assessment is an important part of treatment planning.

Theatre learning

Review positioning, imaging access, implant selection concepts and equipment preparation in accordance with the surgical plan.

Upper limb

Humeral Fractures

Explore proximal humerus, shaft and distal humerus injuries.

Learning objectives

  • Identify the proximal, shaft and distal humerus.
  • Understand common fracture patterns.
  • Explore broad fixation principles.
  • Learn relevant neurovascular anatomy.

Educational concepts

Humeral fractures vary according to anatomical location and complexity. Assessment includes fracture characteristics, joint involvement and relevant neurovascular structures.

Theatre learning

Review patient positioning, imaging access, implant familiarity and the importance of understanding the relevant anatomy.

Lower limb

Ankle Fractures

Understand ankle anatomy, malleolar fractures and stability concepts.

Learning objectives

  • Identify the medial, lateral and posterior malleoli.
  • Understand common ankle fracture classifications.
  • Explore ankle stability concepts.
  • Recognise associated soft tissue injuries.

Educational concepts

Ankle fractures may involve one or multiple malleoli. Assessment considers fracture morphology, joint congruency, stability and associated injuries.

Theatre learning

Study positioning, imaging, implant concepts and equipment preparation for the planned procedure.

Upper limb

Forearm Fractures

Explore radius and ulna anatomy and forearm fracture concepts.

Learning objectives

  • Identify radius and ulna anatomy.
  • Understand common forearm fracture patterns.
  • Explore the importance of forearm rotation.
  • Recognise associated joint injuries.

Educational concepts

Forearm injuries may affect the radius, ulna or both bones. Understanding alignment, rotation and associated joint relationships is important in fracture education.

Theatre learning

Review relevant anatomy, positioning, imaging requirements and implant concepts.
